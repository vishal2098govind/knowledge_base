## Cryptography can do much more
### Digital Signatures:
- Analog of signature in physical world
- In Physical world, when we sign a document, essentially, we write our signature on that document
- Our signature is always the same on all documents
- In digital world, this can't possibly work
	- if attacker obtained one signed document from us, they can cut and paste the signature unto some other document that we might not have wanted to sign
- In digital world, the signature cannot be same for all documents
- In digital world, the **digital signature** are made a **function of content being signed**. So attacker who tries to copy signature from one document to another is not going to succeed.
