## A Rigorous Science
Every concept we describe in crypto is going to follow the following three very rigorous steps
### 1. Precisely specify threat model
- What an attacker could do to attack a digital signature for example
- What is the goal in forging signature for example
### 2. Propose a construction

### 3. Prove that breaking construction under threat mode will solve an underlying hard problem
- Prove that if any attacker that's able to attack the construction under this threat model, 
- that attacker will also have to solve an underlying hard problem, 
- as a result, if the problem is really hard, that proves that no attacker can break the construction under the threat model
