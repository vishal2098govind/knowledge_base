- In general, bucket owners pay for all S3 storage and data transfer costs associated with their bucket
- **With Requester Pays buckets**, the requester instead of bucket owner, pays the cost of request and data downloaded from the bucket
	- Owner still pays storage cost
- Helpful for sharing large data sets with other accounts
- For that, requester must be authenticated in AWS. (cannot be anonymous)
