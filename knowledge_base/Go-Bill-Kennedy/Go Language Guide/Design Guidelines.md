## Prepare your mind

### Somewhere along the line
- We became impressed with the programs with large amount of code
- We strived to create large abstractions in our code base
- We forgot that hardware is the platform
- We have lost the understanding that every decision comes with a cost
	- Engineering is all about understanding the cost associated with the decisions being taken 
### These days are gone
- Where we can throw more hardware/developers at the problem
### Open your mind
- Technology grows faster than change in people's mind
- Easy to adopt new technology but hard to adopt new ways of thinking
